How it works

This tool uses an open-source AI segmentation model that runs directly in your browser with WebAssembly or WebGPU. The first time you use it, your browser downloads the model once (it's cached after that). Then each photo is processed on your device in a few seconds. Nothing is uploaded to a server.

Good for

  • Product photos for Etsy, eBay, Shopify and Amazon listings
  • Profile pictures and headshots
  • Logos, stickers and thumbnails
  • Presentations and social posts

Frequently asked questions

Is it really free?

Yes. No sign-up, no watermark and no limit on the number of images.

Are my photos private?

Yes. The AI model runs in your browser, so your images are never sent anywhere.

Why is the first image slow?

Your browser downloads the AI model the first time. After that it's cached, and later images are much faster.

What resolution do I get?

The download is the same size as your original image.
