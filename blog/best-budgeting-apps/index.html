The Best Budgeting Apps (and a Free Spreadsheet Alternative)

5 November 2026 · 1 min read

The best budget is the one you actually look at. Apps make that easier by pulling in transactions automatically, but they're not the only option. Here's how the main approaches compare.

YNAB: best for changing habits

YNAB (You Need A Budget) uses zero-based budgeting: every unit of money you have gets a job before you spend it. It's especially good for irregular incomes because you only budget money that has already arrived. There's a learning curve, but many users say it changes how they think about money. It offers a free trial.

Monarch Money: best for couples and the big picture

Monarch Money connects to your accounts, categorises spending automatically and shows net worth, investments and goals in one dashboard. You can share it with a partner. Good if you want visibility without much manual work.

Spreadsheets: free and fully yours

A spreadsheet takes a few minutes a week to update, but you control every category, there's no subscription and no bank connection. Many people find the manual step makes them more aware of what they spend.
