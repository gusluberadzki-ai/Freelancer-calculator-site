Best Invoicing Software for Freelancers in 2026

Good invoicing software pays for itself the first time it chases a late client for you. But if you send three invoices a month, you may not need to pay for anything at all. Here's how the main options compare, and who each one suits.

FreshBooks: the easiest all-rounder

FreshBooks was built for service businesses, and it shows. You can track time against a project, turn the hours into an invoice in a couple of clicks, and send automatic reminders when a payment is overdue. Clients can pay by card or bank transfer from the invoice. It's not the cheapest option, but if you bill by the hour it saves the most admin.

Wave: free accounting and invoicing

Wave offers unlimited invoices and basic accounting free, and makes its money from payment processing and paid add-ons. It's a strong choice if you're in the US or Canada and want income and expenses in one place without a subscription. Outside those countries, check which features are available to you.

Zoho Invoice: the best free plan

Zoho Invoice is free, with recurring invoices, payment reminders, time tracking, multiple currencies and a client portal. For a solo freelancer it's hard to beat on price. The trade-off is a busier interface than FreshBooks.

Bonsai: contracts plus invoices

Bonsai combines proposals, contracts, invoices and basic bookkeeping. A client accepts a proposal, signs the contract and pays the deposit in one flow. If you sell projects rather than hours, that flow is worth paying for.

QuickBooks: when you want real books

QuickBooks is overkill for invoicing alone, but if you're registering as a company, employing someone or want your accountant to log in directly, it's the standard many accountants already use.

Or start completely free

If you send only a few invoices a month, the free RateKit invoice generator makes a professional PDF invoice in your browser with no account. Pair it with the late payment calculator to work out interest when a client pays late.

What to look for

  • Online payments: invoices with a "pay now" button get paid faster.
  • Automatic reminders: so you never have to write the awkward email yourself.
  • Multi-currency: essential if you work with clients abroad. Pair it with a low-fee account like Wise to avoid poor exchange rates.
  • Exports for tax time: a clean CSV or report your accountant can use.
