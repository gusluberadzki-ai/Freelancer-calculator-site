# TickBill: Chrome Web Store listing

**Name:** TickBill: Freelance Time & Earnings Tracker
**Category:** Productivity > Tools
**Summary (132 chars max):** One-click timer for freelancers. Track hours per client, watch your earnings grow, and export timesheets to CSV.

**Description:**
TickBill is the simplest way for freelancers and contractors to track billable time.

- One-click start and stop from your toolbar
- Set an hourly rate per client and watch what you've earned update live
- See this week's hours and earnings per client at a glance
- Add a note to every time entry
- Export a CSV timesheet for invoicing, or import it into Excel or Google Sheets
- Works in 7 currencies
- Private: all data stays in your browser. No account, no tracking

**Single purpose (for review):** Track time spent on client work and calculate earnings.
**Permission justification, storage:** Saves the user's clients, rates and time entries locally.
**Data usage:** Does not collect user data.
