Frequently asked questions

How many days are between two dates?

Pick the start and end dates above. By default the end date isn't counted, which is how most deadlines and contracts work. Tick the box to include it.

What counts as a working day?

Monday to Friday. Public holidays aren't removed because they differ by country.

What date is 90 days from today?

Enter 90 in the add-days box and the date appears instantly.
