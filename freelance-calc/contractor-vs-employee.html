Why contractors charge more

A salaried employee gets paid holidays, sick pay, pension contributions and often health cover. A contractor pays for all of that and also has quiet weeks between contracts. That's why a contract day rate that looks high is often worth about the same as a normal salary.

A common rule of thumb is that the day rate should be at least salary ÷ 200 to break even, and salary ÷ 150 to come out clearly ahead.

Frequently asked questions

Is contracting worth it?

If your contract rate is well above the equivalent rate shown here, usually yes. If it's close, the extra risk and admin may not be worth it.

Does this account for tax differences?

No. Tax treatment of contractors differs a lot between countries, so compare after-tax figures with an accountant before deciding.
