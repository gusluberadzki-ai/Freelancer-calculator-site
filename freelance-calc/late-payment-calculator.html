How late payment interest works

Simple interest is calculated as amount × annual rate × days overdue ÷ 365. Many countries give businesses a legal right to charge interest on late B2B invoices even if the contract doesn't mention it, often at a central bank rate plus a few percent. Check the rules where you and your client are based.

Getting paid faster

State your payment terms and late-payment interest on every invoice, send a friendly reminder a few days before the due date, and follow up the day after it passes.

Frequently asked questions

Can I charge interest if it's not in my contract?

In many places yes for business-to-business invoices, but rules vary. Putting it in your terms removes any doubt.

Should I actually charge it?

It's often more useful as leverage: mentioning it in a reminder gets many invoices paid quickly.
