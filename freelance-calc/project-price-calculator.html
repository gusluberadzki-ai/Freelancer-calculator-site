How to price a project

Estimate the hours, add the revision rounds you'll include, then add a contingency buffer. Projects almost always take longer than planned, and a 15 to 25% buffer protects your hourly rate. Round the final number: clients find $3,250 more natural than $3,217.

Protect yourself

Write the number of revision rounds into your contract, and charge your hourly rate for anything beyond them. Ask for a 30 to 50% deposit before starting.

Frequently asked questions

How much buffer should I add to a quote?

15 to 25% for familiar work, 30% or more for something new or vaguely defined.

Should I show the hourly breakdown to the client?

Usually not. Quote the project price and the deliverables, so the conversation is about value rather than hours.
