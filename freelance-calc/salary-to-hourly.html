The quick formula

For a standard 40-hour week with 52 paid weeks, a year has 2,080 working hours. Divide the salary by 2,080 to get the hourly wage, or multiply an hourly wage by 2,080 to get the annual salary. A quick mental shortcut: halve the salary and drop the thousands. $50,000 a year is roughly $25 an hour.

Going freelance?

An employee's hourly wage is not the same as a freelance rate. Freelancers pay for their own holidays, equipment and taxes, so they typically need 1.5 to 2 times the equivalent hourly wage. Use the contractor vs employee calculator to compare properly.

Frequently asked questions

How many working hours are in a year?

2,080 at 40 hours a week for 52 weeks. If you only count weeks actually worked (for example 47 after holidays), it's 1,880.

Does this include tax?

No. It converts gross pay. Your take-home pay depends on where you live.
