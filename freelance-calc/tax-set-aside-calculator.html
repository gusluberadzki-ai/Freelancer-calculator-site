The simple system

Open a separate savings account just for tax. Every time a client pays you, move the amount above into it straight away. When your tax bill arrives, the money is already there.

What rate should I use?

Use your marginal rate plus any social security or health contributions. If you don't know it, 25 to 30% is a cautious starting point in most countries. The buffer covers surprises, and anything left over at the end of the year is a bonus.

This is a planning tool, not tax advice. Check your actual rates with a local accountant.

Frequently asked questions

What percentage should a freelancer save for taxes?

Commonly 25 to 35% of profit, depending on country and income level.

Do I pay tax on the full invoice?

Usually only on profit, which is income minus allowable business expenses.
