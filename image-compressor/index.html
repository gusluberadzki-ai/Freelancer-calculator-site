Free image compressor and resizer

Make photos smaller for websites, email and online listings. Everything happens in your browser, so your images are never uploaded.

How much should I compress?

For photos on a website, JPG or WebP at 70 to 85% quality and 1,600 to 2,000 pixels wide usually looks identical to the original at a fraction of the size. For email attachments, 1,280 pixels wide is plenty. Use PNG for screenshots, logos and graphics with sharp edges or transparency.

Frequently asked questions

Are my images uploaded?

No. Compression runs entirely in your browser using your device, so your photos stay private.

Is there a file limit?

No limit on the number of images. Very large photos may take a second or two each on older phones.

Does it remove photo metadata?

Yes. Re-encoding the image strips EXIF data such as camera details and GPS location, which is good for privacy.
