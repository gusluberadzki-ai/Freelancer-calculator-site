How to make an invoice

A professional invoice needs your business details, the client's details, a unique invoice number, the invoice and due dates, a list of what you did with prices, any tax, the total and how to pay you. Fill in each section above and the preview updates as you type.

Frequently asked questions

Is this invoice generator really free?

Yes. You can create and download as many invoices as you like. Pro is an optional $5 upgrade that removes the small RateKit line and adds saved clients.

Is my data stored anywhere?

No. Everything runs in your browser. Your business details are remembered on this device only, so you don't have to retype them.

How do I save the invoice as a PDF?

Click Download PDF and choose "Save as PDF" in the print window. This works in every modern browser, on desktop and mobile.
