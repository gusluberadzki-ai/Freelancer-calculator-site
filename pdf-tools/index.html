Merge and split PDF files

Combine PDFs, split one into pages, pull out the pages you need, or rotate them. Everything runs in your browser, so your documents stay private.

Private by design

Most online PDF tools upload your documents to their servers. This one processes everything locally in your browser using the open-source pdf-lib library, so contracts, invoices and ID documents never leave your device.
