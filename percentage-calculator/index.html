Percentage formulas

  • X% of Y = Y × X ÷ 100
  • X as a percent of Y = X ÷ Y × 100
  • Percentage change = (new − old) ÷ old × 100
  • Increase Y by X% = Y × (1 + X ÷ 100)

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate a percentage of a number?

Multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. For example, 15% of 200 is 200 × 15 ÷ 100 = 30.

How do I work out percentage change?

Subtract the old value from the new one, divide by the old value, and multiply by 100. Going from 80 to 100 is a 25% increase.

Is a 50% increase then a 50% decrease the same number?

No. 100 increased by 50% is 150, and 150 decreased by 50% is 75.
