Questions

How do I unlock Pro?

Your Gumroad receipt has an unlock link. Open it once on each device and browser you use. Pro then switches on in every RateKit app at the same time.

Can I cancel?

Yes, any time, in one click from the "Manage membership" link on your receipt. You keep every template you downloaded.

Do my files or data go anywhere?

No. RateKit apps run entirely in your browser. Your invoices and resumes never leave your device.

Do the spreadsheets work in Google Sheets?

Yes. They are standard .xlsx files that open in Excel, Google Sheets, Numbers and LibreOffice.
