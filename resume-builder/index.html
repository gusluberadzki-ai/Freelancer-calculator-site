How to write a resume that gets interviews

Keep it to one page unless you have more than ten years of relevant experience. Start with a short profile that says what you do and what you're looking for. Under each job, write two to four bullet points that start with a verb and include a number where you can, such as "Cut monthly reporting time by 40%". List the skills from the job advert that you really have, using the same words, because applicant tracking systems (ATS) match on keywords.

Resume or CV: what's the difference?

In the US a resume is a 1 to 2 page summary and a CV is a long academic record. In the UK, Europe and most other places "CV" means the same as a resume. This tool makes both.
