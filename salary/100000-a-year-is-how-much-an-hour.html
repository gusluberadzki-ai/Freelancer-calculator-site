$100,000 a year is how much an hour?

$100,000 a year is $48.08 an hour, based on a 40-hour week and 52 paid weeks (2,080 hours).

Freelancing instead?

To earn the same as a $100,000 salary as a freelancer, you'd typically need to charge around $72 to $96 an hour, because freelancers pay for their own holidays, equipment and benefits and can't bill every hour. Get an exact figure with the contractor vs employee calculator.
