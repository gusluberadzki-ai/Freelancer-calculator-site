About BMI

BMI is weight divided by height squared. It's a quick screening number for adults, but it doesn't account for muscle mass, age, sex or ethnicity, so talk to a doctor for a proper assessment.

Frequently asked questions

What is a healthy BMI?

For most adults, 18.5 to 24.9 is considered a healthy range.

Is BMI accurate for athletes?

Not always. Muscle weighs more than fat, so muscular people can have a high BMI without excess body fat.
