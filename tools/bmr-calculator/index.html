What is BMR?

Basal metabolic rate is the energy your body uses at complete rest just to keep you alive: breathing, circulation and keeping warm. It's usually 60–70% of the calories you burn in a day. This calculator uses the Mifflin-St Jeor equation, which studies have found to be the most accurate of the common formulas.

Frequently asked questions

Is BMR the same as TDEE?

No. TDEE (total daily energy expenditure) is BMR multiplied by an activity factor. Both are shown above.

Is this medical advice?

No. It's an estimate. Speak to a doctor or dietitian before major diet changes.
