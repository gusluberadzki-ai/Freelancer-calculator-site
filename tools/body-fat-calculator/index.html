Body fat calculator

Estimate your body fat percentage with a tape measure.

–

How to measure

Measure your neck just below the larynx, and your waist at the navel for men or at the narrowest point for women. Women also measure the widest part of the hips. Keep the tape level and snug but not tight. The Navy method is usually within 3–4% of lab methods.

Frequently asked questions

How accurate is it?

Reasonably accurate for most people, but it can be off for very muscular or very lean bodies. Use it to track change over time.
