How break-even works

Each sale contributes its price minus its variable cost towards your fixed costs. Divide the fixed costs by that contribution and you get the number of sales needed to break even.

Frequently asked questions

What are fixed costs?

Costs that don't change with sales volume, like rent, software subscriptions and salaries.
