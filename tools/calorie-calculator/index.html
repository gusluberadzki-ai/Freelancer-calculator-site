BMR and TDEE

BMR is what your body burns at rest. TDEE multiplies it by your activity level to estimate what you burn in a normal day. This uses the Mifflin-St Jeor equation, which is among the most accurate for most adults.

Frequently asked questions

Is this medical advice?

No. It's an estimate. Talk to a doctor or dietitian before a big change, especially if you have a health condition.

How fast should I lose weight?

About 0.25–0.5 kg (0.5–1 lb) a week is a common, sustainable target.
