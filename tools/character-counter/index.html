Why limits matter

Search engines cut off titles after about 60 characters and descriptions after about 160, and social platforms truncate or reject posts that are too long. Check before you publish.

Frequently asked questions

Do emoji count as one character?

This counter counts each emoji as one character, which matches what most platforms show you.
