HEX, RGB and HSL

HEX and RGB describe the same thing: amounts of red, green and blue from 0 to 255. HSL describes hue (0–360°), saturation and lightness, which is easier for making lighter or darker versions of a colour.

Frequently asked questions

What contrast ratio do I need?

WCAG AA asks for at least 4.5:1 for normal text and 3:1 for large text.
