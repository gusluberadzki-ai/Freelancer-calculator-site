Typical depths

Patios and paths are usually 7–10 cm (3–4 in) deep. Driveways and garage floors are usually 10–15 cm (4–6 in). For large jobs above about 1 m³, ready-mix delivery is often cheaper than bags.

Frequently asked questions

How many 20 kg bags make a cubic metre?

About 100. A 20 kg bag of premix yields roughly 0.01 m³.

How many cubic yards in a cubic metre?

One cubic metre is 1.308 cubic yards.
