Stacked discounts don't add up

25% off followed by an extra 10% off is not 35% off. The second discount applies to the already reduced price, so the total discount is 32.5%.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate a percentage off?

Multiply the price by (1 − discount ÷ 100). 25% off $80 is $80 × 0.75 = $60.
