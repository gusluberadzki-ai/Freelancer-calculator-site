How due dates are estimated

This uses Naegele's rule: 280 days (40 weeks) from the first day of your last period, adjusted for cycles longer or shorter than 28 days. Only about 1 in 25 babies arrive on their due date, so treat it as a guide.

Frequently asked questions

Is an ultrasound date more accurate?

Yes. An early dating scan is usually more accurate than a date from your last period. Follow your midwife or doctor's date.
