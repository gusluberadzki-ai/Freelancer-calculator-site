Electricity cost

See what any appliance costs to run, from its wattage and your electricity price.

–

How it works

Cost = power in kilowatts × hours used × price per kWh. A 2,000 W heater used for one hour uses 2 kWh. The wattage is printed on the appliance label or in the manual. Devices on standby still draw a few watts.

Frequently asked questions

Where do I find my electricity price?

It's on your bill or your supplier's app, shown as a price per kWh (unit rate).
