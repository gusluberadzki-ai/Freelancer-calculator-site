Emergency fund

Work out your emergency fund target and how long it will take to build.

–

How big should it be?

Three to six months of essential costs (rent, food, bills, minimum debt payments) is the usual advice. Freelancers and people with one household income should aim higher, because income can stop without warning. Keep it in an easy-access savings account, not invested.

Frequently asked questions

What counts as essential?

Housing, utilities, groceries, insurance, transport and minimum debt payments. Leave out subscriptions and eating out.
