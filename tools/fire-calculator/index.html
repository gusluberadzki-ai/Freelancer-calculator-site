The idea

FIRE stands for financial independence, retire early. Your FIRE number is about 25 times yearly spending (a 4% withdrawal rate). The biggest lever is your savings rate: the more you save and the less you spend, the faster you get there.

Frequently asked questions

Why use a real return?

Using a return after inflation keeps everything in today's money, so the FIRE number stays meaningful.
