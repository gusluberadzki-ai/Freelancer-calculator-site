Fraction calculator

Add, subtract, multiply or divide two fractions.

How it works

To add or subtract fractions, they're put over a common denominator first. To multiply, multiply the tops and the bottoms. To divide, flip the second fraction and multiply. The result is then simplified by dividing by the greatest common divisor.

Frequently asked questions

How do I enter a mixed number?

Convert it to an improper fraction first: 2 1/3 is (2×3+1)/3 = 7/3.
