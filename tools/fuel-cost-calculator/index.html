How it works

Fuel used is the distance times your car's consumption. With litres per 100 km, distance is in kilometres and the price is per litre. With miles per gallon, distance is in miles and the price is per gallon.

Frequently asked questions

Where do I find my car's fuel economy?

Check the trip computer, the manual, or the official figure for your model. Real-world economy is usually a little worse than the official figure.
