Grade calculator

Find out what you need on the final exam to get the grade you want.

–

The formula

Needed = (wanted − current × (1 − weight)) ÷ weight. With 82% now, a final worth 30% and a target of 85%, you need 92%.

Frequently asked questions

What if my current grade already includes some weight?

Enter your current grade as the average over everything except the final, and the final's share of the whole course.
