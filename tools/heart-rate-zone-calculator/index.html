The method

Max heart rate is estimated with the Tanaka formula (208 − 0.7 × age), which is more accurate than 220 − age. Zones use the Karvonen method, which takes your resting heart rate into account so the zones fit your fitness level.

Frequently asked questions

How do I find my resting heart rate?

Count your pulse for 60 seconds just after waking, before getting up. Average it over a few days.
