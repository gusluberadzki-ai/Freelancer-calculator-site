Hours calculator

Add up hours worked from start and end times, minus breaks, and see your pay.

–

How to use it

Enter a start time, end time and unpaid break in minutes for each day. Leave a day blank if you didn't work. Night shifts that end after midnight are handled automatically. Decimal hours (for example 7.5) are what most payroll and invoicing systems need.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert minutes to decimal hours?

Divide the minutes by 60. 45 minutes is 0.75 hours, so 7h 45m is 7.75 hours.

Does it handle overnight shifts?

Yes. If the end time is earlier than the start time, it assumes the shift ran past midnight.
