There's no single ideal weight

These formulas were designed for medication dosing and population studies, not as personal targets. Muscle, frame size and age all matter. The BMI range of 18.5 to 24.9 is the broadest guide.

Which formula is best?

None is definitive. The BMI range is the most widely used; the others give a single point estimate.
