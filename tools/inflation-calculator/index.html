Why inflation matters

At 3% inflation prices roughly double every 24 years. Cash that earns less than inflation loses real value each year, which is why long-term savings usually need to be invested.

Frequently asked questions

What inflation rate should I use?

Central banks in many countries target about 2%. Long-run averages have been around 3%. Use a range to see the effect.
