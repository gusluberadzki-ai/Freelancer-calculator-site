How loan payments are calculated

This uses the standard amortisation formula: payment = P × r ÷ (1 − (1 + r)−n), where P is the amount borrowed, r is the monthly interest rate and n is the number of monthly payments. Early payments are mostly interest; later payments are mostly principal.

Frequently asked questions

How can I lower my monthly payment?

Choose a longer term or find a lower interest rate. A longer term lowers the payment but increases the total interest you pay.

Does this include fees?

Add any upfront fees in the fees box to see the total cost. Ongoing fees vary by lender and aren't included.
