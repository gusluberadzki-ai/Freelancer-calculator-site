Calories per gram

Protein and carbohydrate both provide about 4 kcal per gram, and fat provides about 9 kcal per gram. The percentages above are shares of calories, not grams. Not sure of your calories? Use the BMR calculator first.

Frequently asked questions

How much protein do I need?

Common guidance for active people is 1.6–2.2 g per kg of body weight per day.
