Morse timing

A dash is three times as long as a dot. The gap between parts of a letter is one dot, between letters three dots, and between words seven dots. The player above uses the standard timing at about 15 words per minute.

How do I separate words in Morse?

Use a slash (/) between words and a single space between letters.
