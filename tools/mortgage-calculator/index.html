What goes into a mortgage payment

Your lender's payment covers principal and interest, calculated with the standard amortisation formula. On top of that most owners pay property tax and home insurance, often collected monthly through an escrow account. A bigger deposit lowers both the loan and the total interest.

Frequently asked questions

How much deposit do I need?

It depends on the lender and country. Many lenders ask for 5–20%. Under 20% often means paying mortgage insurance, which isn't included here.

Should I choose 15 or 30 years?

A 15-year term has higher payments but far less total interest. Try both terms above to compare.
