Overtime rules

In the US, the Fair Labor Standards Act requires time and a half for non-exempt employees after 40 hours in a work week. Some states, such as California, also require daily overtime. In other countries overtime rules depend on your contract.

Frequently asked questions

Is overtime calculated per day or per week?

Federally in the US it's per week. Some states and contracts also have daily thresholds.
