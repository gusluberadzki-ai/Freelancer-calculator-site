Paint calculator

Estimate how much paint you need to cover the walls of a room.

–

Assumptions

One litre of emulsion covers about 11 m² and one US gallon about 375 sq ft per coat on smooth walls. A standard door takes off about 1.9 m² (20 sq ft) and a window about 1.5 m² (15 sq ft). Rough or porous surfaces and dark-to-light colour changes need more paint.

Frequently asked questions

How many coats do I need?

Two coats is standard for an even finish. New plaster or a big colour change may need a primer as well.

Does this include the ceiling?

No. The ceiling estimate is shown separately.
