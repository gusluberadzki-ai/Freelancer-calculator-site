Dog and cat age

Convert your dog's or cat's age into human years.

–

Why not just multiply by 7?

Pets grow up very fast in their first two years: a one-year-old dog or cat is roughly like a 15-year-old person, and a two-year-old like a 24-year-old. After that each year counts for about 4 to 7 human years, with larger dogs ageing faster.

Frequently asked questions

Do big dogs age faster?

Yes. Large and giant breeds have shorter lifespans and are considered senior from around 6–8 years old.
