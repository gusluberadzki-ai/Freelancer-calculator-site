How the Pomodoro technique works

Work on one task for 25 minutes, then take a 5-minute break. After four focus sessions, take a longer 15 to 30 minute break. The timer keeps running in the tab title so you can see it from other tabs.

Frequently asked questions

Will it make a sound?

Yes, a short beep plays when each session ends. Keep the tab open.
