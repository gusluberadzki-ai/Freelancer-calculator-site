Margin vs markup

Margin is profit as a share of the selling price. Markup is profit as a share of the cost. Something that costs $40 and sells for $100 has a 60% margin but a 150% markup.

Frequently asked questions

What's a good profit margin?

It varies hugely by industry. Digital products can run 80–90% margins; retail is often 20–50%.
