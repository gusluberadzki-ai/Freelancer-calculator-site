Using ratios

Ratios keep proportions the same when scaling. A 16:9 image that's 1920 pixels wide is 1080 pixels tall. A recipe for 4 that needs 300 g flour needs 450 g for 6 people.

Frequently asked questions

How do I simplify a ratio?

Divide both sides by their greatest common divisor. 1920:1080 divided by 120 is 16:9.
