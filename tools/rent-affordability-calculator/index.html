Three rules of thumb

The 30% rule says rent should be no more than 30% of gross income. Many US landlords require yearly income of at least 40 times the monthly rent. The 50/30/20 budget puts all needs, including rent and minimum debt payments, at 50% of take-home pay, which is the most conservative.
