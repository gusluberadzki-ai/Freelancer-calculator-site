The 4% rule

A common rule of thumb says you can withdraw about 4% of your pot in the first year of retirement and adjust for inflation after that, with a good chance the money lasts 30 years. It's a guide, not a guarantee.

Frequently asked questions

Are these figures in today's money?

No, they're in future money. To think in today's money, use a return that's about 2–3 points lower to allow for inflation.

Does this include a state pension?

No. Add any state or workplace pension income on top.
