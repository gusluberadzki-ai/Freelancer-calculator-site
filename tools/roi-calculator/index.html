ROI vs annualised return

ROI tells you the total gain. Annualised return (CAGR) spreads that over the years you held the investment, which lets you compare investments held for different lengths of time.

Frequently asked questions

What is CAGR?

Compound annual growth rate: the steady yearly rate that would turn your starting amount into the final value over the same period.
