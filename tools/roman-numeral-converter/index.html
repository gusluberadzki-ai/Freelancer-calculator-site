The rules

I = 1, V = 5, X = 10, L = 50, C = 100, D = 500, M = 1000. A smaller numeral before a larger one is subtracted (IV = 4, XC = 90). Standard numerals never repeat a symbol more than three times.

Frequently asked questions

Why stop at 3999?

Standard Roman numerals have no symbol above M (1000), so 3999 (MMMCMXCIX) is the largest you can write without special notation.
