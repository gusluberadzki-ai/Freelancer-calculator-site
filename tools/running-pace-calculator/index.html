Race predictions

Predicted times use Riegel's formula: T2 = T1 × (D2 ÷ D1)1.06. It works best when you've trained for the longer distance.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert pace per km to per mile?

Multiply the pace by 1.609. A 5:30 /km pace is about 8:51 per mile.
