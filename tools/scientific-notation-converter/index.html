Convert a number to scientific notation, or back to a plain number.

The format

Scientific notation writes a number as a value between 1 and 10 multiplied by a power of ten. 299,792,458 becomes 2.998 × 10⁸. Engineering notation uses powers that are multiples of three, which match units like kilo, mega and micro.

Frequently asked questions

What is E notation?

A way of typing scientific notation: 6.02e23 means 6.02 × 10²³.
