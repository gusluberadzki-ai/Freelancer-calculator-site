Why sleep cycles matter

Sleep runs in cycles of about 90 minutes. Waking at the end of a cycle, rather than in deep sleep, usually leaves you feeling less groggy. Most adults need 5 or 6 cycles a night.

Frequently asked questions

Is 90 minutes exact?

No. Cycles vary from about 80 to 110 minutes between people and through the night, so use this as a starting point.
