What makes a good slug

Keep it short, lowercase and readable, with words separated by hyphens. Google treats hyphens as word separators but not underscores, so hyphens are the usual choice.

Frequently asked questions

Should I remove words like 'the' and 'a'?

Often, yes. Shorter slugs are easier to read and share. Keep the main keyword.
