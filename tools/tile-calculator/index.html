How much waste to allow

Add 10% for a simple rectangular room with straight laying. Add 15% for diagonal or herringbone patterns, or a room with lots of corners and cuts. Keep a few spare tiles for future repairs, since batches vary in colour.

Frequently asked questions

Should I include the grout gap?

Yes. Gaps add up over a large area and slightly reduce the number of tiles you need.
