What is Unix time?

Unix time counts the seconds since 1 January 1970 at 00:00 UTC. It's how most computers and databases store dates. JavaScript and some APIs use milliseconds instead, which this tool detects automatically.

Frequently asked questions

Seconds or milliseconds?

Timestamps with 13 digits are milliseconds; 10 digits are seconds. The converter detects which one you entered.
