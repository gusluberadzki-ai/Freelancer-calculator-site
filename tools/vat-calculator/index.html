Removing VAT correctly

To remove 20% VAT from a price, divide by 1.20. A common mistake is to subtract 20% of the gross price, which gives the wrong answer: £120 includes £20 VAT, not £24.

Frequently asked questions

How do I add VAT to a price?

Multiply the net price by 1 plus the rate. At 20%, multiply by 1.2.

Does this work for US sales tax?

Yes. Enter your combined state and local rate.
