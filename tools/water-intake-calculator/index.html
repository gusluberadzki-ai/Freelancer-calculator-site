Water intake

Get a daily water target based on your body weight and exercise.

–

How it's worked out

A common guide is about 33 ml per kg of body weight, plus around 350 ml for every 30 minutes of exercise, and extra in hot weather. Food provides some of your water too.

Frequently asked questions

Do tea and coffee count?

Yes, mostly. Water-rich food like fruit and soup also counts towards your intake.
